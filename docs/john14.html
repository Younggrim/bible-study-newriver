1“Do not let your hearts be troubled. You believe in God; believe in Me as well.

2In My Father’s house are many rooms. If it were not so, would I have told you that I am going there to prepare a place for you?

3And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come back and welcome you into My presence, so that you also may be where I am.

4You know the way to the place where I am going.”

5“Lord,” said Thomas, “we do not know where You are going, so how can we know the way?”

6Jesus answered, “I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through Me.

7If you had known Me, you would know My Father as well. From now on you do know Him and have seen Him.”

8Philip said to Him, “Lord, show us the Father, and that will be enough for us.”

9Jesus replied, “Philip, I have been with you all this time, and still you do not know Me? Anyone who has seen Me has seen the Father. How can you say, ‘Show us the Father’?

10Do you not believe that I am in the Father and the Father is in Me? The words I say to you, I do not speak on My own. Instead, it is the Father dwelling in Me, performing His works.

11Believe Me that I am in the Father and the Father is in Me—or at least believe on account of the works themselves.

12Truly, truly, I tell you, whoever believes in Me will also do the works that I am doing. He will do even greater things than these, because I am going to the Father.

13And I will do whatever you ask in My name, so that the Father may be glorified in the Son.

14If you ask Me for anything in My name, I will do it.

15If you love Me, you will keep My commandments.

16And I will ask the Father, and He will give you another Advocate to be with you forever—

17the Spirit of truth. The world cannot receive Him, because it neither sees Him nor knows Him. But you do know Him, for He abides with you and will be in you.

18I will not leave you as orphans; I will come to you.

19In a little while the world will see Me no more, but you will see Me. Because I live, you also will live.

20On that day you will know that I am in My Father, and you are in Me, and I am in you.

21Whoever has My commandments and keeps them is the one who loves Me. The one who loves Me will be loved by My Father, and I will love him and reveal Myself to him.”

22Judas (not Iscariot) asked Him, “Lord, why are You going to reveal Yourself to us and not to the world?”

23Jesus replied, “If anyone loves Me, he will keep My word. My Father will love him, and We will come to him and make Our home with him.

24Whoever does not love Me does not keep My words. The word that you hear is not My own, but it is from the Father who sent Me.

25All this I have spoken to you while I am still with you.

26But the Advocate, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in My name, will teach you all things and will remind you of everything I have told you.

27Peace I leave with you; My peace I give to you. I do not give to you as the world gives. Do not let your hearts be troubled; do not be afraid.

28You heard Me say, ‘I am going away, and I am coming back to you.’ If you loved Me, you would rejoice that I am going to the Father, because the Father is greater than I.

29And now I have told you before it happens, so that when it does happen, you will believe.

30I will not speak with you much longer, for the prince of this world is coming, and he has no claim on Me.

31But I do exactly what the Father has commanded Me, so that the world may know that I love the Father. Get up! Let us go on from here.

Summary

Jesus comforts the disciples ahead of His departure, telling them not to be troubled and promising that in His Father's house are many rooms, where He is going to prepare a place for them and will return to take them there (vv. 1-3). When Thomas asks how to know the way, Jesus declares that He himself is the way, the truth, and the life, and that no one comes to the Father except through Him (vv. 5-6). He answers Philip that to have seen Him is to have seen the Father (vv. 8-9), then promises that after He leaves, the Father will send another Counselor, the Spirit of truth, to remain with believers forever (vv. 16-17). Responding to Judas (not Iscariot), Jesus explains that those who love Him and keep His word will be indwelt by Him and the Father, and that the coming Spirit will teach them all things (vv. 22-26). He leaves them His peace, unlike the world's peace, telling them not to be troubled or afraid (v. 27), then closes by affirming His love-driven obedience to the Father before urging them to rise and go (vv. 30-31).

Authorship & Background

Author: The Apostle John (see Chapter 1 notes for full authorship details).
Historical Context: Chapter 14 is part of the Upper Room Discourse (chapters 13-17), spoken on the night of Jesus' arrest. The disciples were troubled — Jesus had just announced His departure, predicted Peter's denial, and identified a betrayer. Chapter 14 is Jesus' direct response to their troubled hearts.
This chapter contains some of the most comforting and theologically rich words in all of Scripture: the promise of heaven (vv.1-3), the exclusive claim of Christ as the only way to the Father (v.6), the promise of the Holy Spirit as Comforter (vv.16-17,26), and the gift of Christ's peace (v.27).
The Holy Spirit (Paraclete): Jesus introduces the Holy Spirit here as the "Comforter" (Greek: Paraclete — one called alongside to help). The Spirit is described as "another Comforter" — the same kind as Jesus, meaning the Spirit is personal, not a force. He will teach, remind, and abide with believers forever.

Map & Geography

  • The Upper Room in Jerusalem: The Upper Room Discourse continues. Jesus speaks to His disciples on the night before the cross.
  • "My Father's house" (v.2): Jesus speaks of heaven as a place with "many mansions" — a destination He is going to prepare for them.
  • "Whither I go ye know, and the way ye know" (v.4): Jesus speaks of His departure from Jerusalem to the Father.

Reflection

  • A Command to Trust (v.1) — Jesus doesn't ask the disciples not to feel troubled — He commands them not to let their hearts stay troubled, calling for a choice to trust. What is troubling your heart right now, and what would it look like to actively choose trust over anxiety?
  • The Way, the Truth, and the Life (v.6) — In a moment of comfort to grieving friends, Jesus makes an exclusive claim: no one comes to the Father except through Him. How do you hold that conviction with both confidence and compassion?
  • Love Shown Through Obedience (v.15) — Jesus ties love for Him directly to keeping His commands, not just feeling affection toward Him. What commandment of Christ are you currently struggling to keep, and what does that reveal about where your love actually is?
  • The Spirit Who Remains (v.26) — Jesus promises the Holy Spirit will teach and remind believers of everything He said, staying with them for good. How present is the Spirit's ongoing teaching in your daily life, not just at conversion?
  • A Different Kind of Peace (v.27) — Christ's peace doesn't depend on good circumstances the way the world's peace does. Are you experiencing His peace right now, or are you waiting for your circumstances to be right before you feel at peace?
