How this site is made

Last updated 17 November 2026

I built this site for my own study and to share with friends. I’m not a pastor or a trained scholar, just a guy trying to grow in God’s Word.

AI helps write the study material

Much of the study material here, including the chapter summaries, background notes and Deep Dives, is written with the help of AI (Claude). It gathers and summarizes what trusted commentators have said. Each Deep Dive names those commentators, and the Commentary tab links to them so you can read them yourself.

None of this is my own original work. I go through it as I study, but I haven’t read every chapter’s notes myself yet.

Where the sources come from

  • Scripture comes from the translations credited at the bottom of every page, used under each publisher’s terms
  • Commentators such as David Guzik, Matthew Henry and Jamieson-Fausset-Brown are named and linked. Guzik’s work is under copyright, so it is only paraphrased, never copied
  • Videos and articles are links to the people who made them, and nothing is copied from them

Scripture is the authority

These notes are a study aid, not the final word. Test everything against Scripture (1 Thessalonians 5:21). If something here doesn’t line up with the Bible, trust the Bible, and please let me know so I can fix it.
