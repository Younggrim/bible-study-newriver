I AM Statements of Jesus

Seven declarations where Jesus reveals His divine identity.

All Subjects

I Am the Bread of Life

Jesus satisfies our deepest spiritual hunger.

I Am the Light of the World

Jesus dispels darkness and reveals truth.

I Am the Door

Jesus is the only entrance to salvation.

I Am the Good Shepherd

Jesus protects, guides, and lays down His life.

I Am the Resurrection and the Life

Jesus conquers death itself.

I Am the Way, the Truth, and the Life

No one comes to the Father except through Him.

I Am the True Vine

Abiding in Christ produces spiritual fruit.
