Fruits of the Spirit

Nine expressions of Spirit-filled character growing in the believer's life.

All Subjects

Love

agape (ἀγάπη)

Unconditional, sacrificial love that mirrors God's love.

Joy

chara (χαρά)

Deep gladness anchored in God, not circumstances.

Peace

eirene (εἰρήνη)

Wholeness and tranquility from being reconciled to God.

Patience

makrothumia (μακροθυμία)

Long-suffering endurance that bears with others.

Kindness

chrestotes (χρηστότης)

A gracious disposition that seeks to bless others.

Goodness

agathosune (ἀγαθωσύνη)

Moral excellence expressed through righteous action.

Faithfulness

pistis (πίστις)

Trustworthiness reflecting God's covenant loyalty.

Gentleness

prautes (πραΰτης)

Strength under control — meekness yielded to God.

Self-Control

egkrateia (ἐγκράτεια)

Mastery over desires through the Holy Spirit.
