Kings of Israel & Judah

The rulers of a divided kingdom — some faithful, many not.

All Subjects

Saul

Israel's first king — started well, ended in ruin.

David

The greatest king — established God's throne forever.

Solomon

Wisest man alive — undone by divided loyalty.

Rehoboam

Arrogance that split a kingdom in two.

Jeroboam

Led Israel into idolatry — 'the sin of Jeroboam.'

Ahab

Most wicked king — married Jezebel, worshiped Baal.

Jehoshaphat

Good king who sought God — but made bad alliances.

Hezekiah

Revival king — trusted God against Assyria.

Josiah

The boy king who found the lost Book of the Law.
