Ten Commandments

God's foundational law for how to love Him and love others.

All Subjects

No Other Gods

Exclusive loyalty to the one true God.

No Idols

Worship God alone — no created images.

Do Not Take God's Name in Vain

Revere God's name and character.

Remember the Sabbath

Rest in God's finished work.

Honor Your Father and Mother

Respect authority structures God established.

You Shall Not Murder

Guard the heart against hatred.

You Shall Not Commit Adultery

Faithfulness in marriage and purity of heart.

You Shall Not Steal

Honest labor and generous giving.

You Shall Not Bear False Witness

Integrity in speech — no deception.

You Shall Not Covet

Contentment in God's provision.
